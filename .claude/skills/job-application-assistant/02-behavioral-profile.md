## Strongest Behaviors
- **Technical Ownership:** Delivers exceptionally well when given clear ownership; "works well with the ownership given" is consistent feedback from managers.
- **Research-First Problem Solving:** Instinctively researches industry standards, alternatives, and prior art before prototyping; structures decisions around data and expert input rather than opinion.
- **Business Impact Framing:** Communicates technical concepts through business impact and analogies, aligning stakeholders effectively.
- **Async-First Execution with Sync Alignment:** Prefers whiteboarding/discussion upfront to get everyone on the same page, then async-written updates and independent execution.
- **Data-Driven Conflict Resolution:** Lists pros/cons, consults experts, and decides on evidence rather than debate.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "High autonomy" / "ownership" / "trust-first culture"
- "Learning culture" / "modern stack" / "engineering-driven"
- "Research-oriented" / "R&D mindset" / "publish or open-source encouraged"
- "Async-first" / "written culture" / "few meetings"
- "Technical depth in leadership" / "IC track" / "staff/principal path"
- "Blameless postmortems" / "psychological safety"
- "Zero-to-one" / "greenfield" / "define the problem, not just solve it"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "Fast-paced environment" (often code for reactive, low-depth)
- "Wear many hats" (can mean unfocused, no research space)
- "Hit the ground running" (may imply no ramp-up for research)
- "Rockstar / ninja / 10x engineer" (signals hero culture, not sustainable ownership)
- "Process-heavy" / "strict agile ceremonies" (conflicts with async-first preference)
- "Micromanagement" / "daily standups with detailed reporting" (direct clash)
- "Arbitrary deadlines" / "fixed scope, fixed time" (conflicts with research-first approach)

## Management Style Preferences
- **Works best with:** High trust + autonomy; regular 1:1s with technical depth; clear OKRs/outcomes with freedom on "how"; mentorship-oriented; blameless culture; engineering-driven prioritization.
- **Frustrated by:** Micromanagement; arbitrary deadlines without technical justification; lack of technical depth in leadership; excessive low-value meetings; hero-culture rewarding firefighting over prevention; silence/ghosting from manager.

For weakness: "I've learned to distinguish signal from noise — early in my career I'd push on everything; now I focus energy where I have leverage and let go of the rest."

Don't imply you need zero structure — you need *technical* structure (clear priorities, technical leadership), not process structure. Don't frame "starting own venture" as leaving soon — frame as "seeking maximum learning velocity on path to technical leadership."
